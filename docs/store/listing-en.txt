Feel the raw speed of a jet fighter.

Your engines roar, the catapult fires, and you're thrown off the deck at full afterburner. Hunt down enemy fighters, break through anti-aircraft fire and sink the enemy carrier — then make it home before your fuel runs dry.

■ LAUNCH, FIGHT, LAND, REPEAT
• Catapult launches straight off your aircraft carrier
• Dogfight enemy jets that keep launching from their carrier
• Lock on and fire homing missiles, or strafe with your cannon
• Sink the enemy carrier in a massive chain of explosions
• Watch your fuel — fly back, catch the arresting wire, refuel, rearm and launch again

■ BUILT FOR SPEED
• Break the sound barrier and punch through a vapor cone
• Speed lines, camera shake and a widening field of view as you accelerate
• Skim the sea and kick up spray at low altitude
• Huge fireballs, sparks, smoke and shockwaves on every hit

■ EASY TO PICK UP
• Simple touch controls: one stick to fly, buttons for gun, missiles, afterburner and brake
• On-screen guidance tells you what to do next — lock on, fire, or head home
• Automatic landing approach when you're near your carrier

■ FEATURES
• Endless stages — each new enemy carrier group is tougher than the last
• Ranks from Cadet to Legendary Ace
• Share your score and rank with friends
• Plays offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
• English and Japanese
